# Writing the script

The art is the easy part. The reel lives or dies on nine short lines.

## Shape

- **Title**: a number and a noun phrase. `8 *forgotten* sunnahs of [Jumuah]`.
  The number is a contract - deliver exactly that many.
- **Item headline**: under 7 words, one idea, imperative or second person.
  `[Walk] to Jumuah`. `Dont [step over] people`.
- **Subline**: the payoff the headline earned. `Every step = *a year* of
  fasting and qiyam`. If it repeats the headline in other words, cut it and
  let the art carry the beat.
- **Recap row**: the headline crushed to about four words. `Walk: a year per
  step`. Not a new sentence.
- **Closing question**: one, open, answerable in a comment. `Which one are
  you bringing back *tomorrow*?` This is what the comments section runs on.

## The highlight

`[...]` is the single most important authoring decision in the scene. It is
where the eye lands and it is what the viewer remembers.

- Mark the **surprising** word, not the subject. `Keep a [separate outfit]`,
  not `[Keep] a separate outfit`.
- Mark the **turn**: `Khutbah [already started?]`.
- One box. Two boxes cancel each other out.
- Never box a whole headline.

## The italic

`*...*` is the soft, specific half of a line: names (`*Surah al-Kahf*`),
quantities (`*a year*`, `*4 rakahs*`), and time (`*tomorrow*`). It is the
voice dropping slightly, not rising.

## Rhythm across the reel

Vary the item shapes or the format flattens:

- two or three items with a `big` number
- two or three with only a headline and art
- one with a `wash` change for time of day
- the hardest-hitting item third or fourth, not first - the first is still
  the hook, the fourth is where a scroller decides to stay

## Tone

- Calm. No exclamation marks, no "Did you know", no "most people don't".
- Say the thing, cite it, move. The restraint is the authority.
- Second person, present tense.
- No guilt. `Dont [step over] people` is an instruction; `Stop ruining
  everyone's Jumuah` is a scolding, and scolding does not get saved or
  shared.
- Write for someone who already agrees and wants the detail, not for someone
  you are arguing with.

## Caption (posted with the reel, not in the video)

Three parts: the list in plain text so it is searchable, the sources, and
the same closing question. Keep the first line under 125 characters so it
survives the "more" fold.
